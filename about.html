The Short Version

I'm Zil. I've been writing software professionally for about five years, mostly full-stack. React and TypeScript on the front, Node and GraphQL behind it, AWS underneath. Right now I build the in-house POS and billing software AMA's agents use, and I co-founded findnaseeb on the side.

What I actually like is the building. Take something vague, figure out how it should work, get it in front of real people. I'm the one who reads the big PR carefully, chases the weird production bug, and cares whether the thing is still simple to run six months later.

Outside work I'm usually building something too. Right now that's a project car that's eaten more weekends than I'll admit. When I'm not under it I'm hiking, traveling, taking photos, or volunteering. A few of those are under Photos.
